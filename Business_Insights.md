# Business Insights — COVID-19 Cases & Vaccination Analysis

## 1. Business Objective

Understand the scale of COVID-19 in India, identify states with higher case counts, examine how cases changed over time, and explore global vaccination progress and recorded vaccine types.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Total Confirmed Cases (India) | **34.3 million** | India's cumulative confirmed cases on the latest available date. |
| Total Recoveries (India) | **33.7 million** | India's cumulative recoveries on the latest available date. |
| Total Deaths (India) | **458 thousand** | India's cumulative deaths on the latest available date. |
| Total Vaccinations (Across Countries) | **8.2 billion** | Total recorded vaccination doses across 228 countries. |
| Average Fully Vaccinated | **43.57%** | Arithmetic average of available country-level fully vaccinated percentages; not population-weighted. |

The first three KPIs represent India's latest cumulative COVID-19 records in the available state-wise data. The vaccination KPIs represent the global country-level vaccination dataset.

## 3. Key Business Findings

### Overall COVID-19 Impact

- **Finding:** India recorded approximately 34.3 million confirmed cases, 33.7 million recoveries, and 458 thousand deaths in the latest available state-wise data.
- **Business Insight:** Reviewing confirmed cases, recoveries, and deaths together provides a broader picture of the pandemic's impact and helps identify periods requiring public-health attention.

### State-wise COVID-19 Cases

- **Finding:** Maharashtra recorded the highest confirmed case count among Indian states, with **6,611,078 confirmed cases** and **6,450,585 recoveries** in the latest state-wise figures.
- **Business Insight:** States with higher confirmed case counts could be prioritized when reviewing healthcare resource requirements and public-health response needs.

### COVID-19 Case Trend

- **Finding:** The line chart shows changes in daily confirmed cases, recoveries, and deaths between January 2020 and October 2021. In **May 2021**, the recorded monthly totals were **9,016,687 confirmed cases, 10,266,501 recoveries, and 120,072 deaths**.
- **Business Insight:** Comparing daily trends over time can help public-health teams identify periods of increased infection activity and review the corresponding healthcare response.

### Vaccination Progress

- **Finding:** The global vaccination dataset recorded approximately **8.2 billion vaccination doses** across 228 countries. The average fully vaccinated percentage across available country records was **43.57%**.
- **Business Insight:** Comparing country-level vaccination percentages can help identify differences in vaccination progress and areas that may require further attention.

### Countries with the Highest Vaccination Counts

- **Finding:** China ranked first in the dashboard's Top 10 Vaccinations visual, with **2,543,206,400 recorded vaccination doses**.
- **Business Insight:** Comparing total vaccination counts shows differences in vaccination volume across countries. Population size should also be considered when comparing vaccination coverage.

### Vaccine Types

- **Finding:** Pfizer-BioNTech – Comirnaty was the most frequently recorded vaccine type in the chart, with **163 country-vaccine entries (19.06%)**. Other recorded types include AstraZeneca – Vaxzevria, Janssen, Moderna, and others.
- **Business Insight:** Reviewing recorded vaccine types provides an overview of the different vaccines listed across countries. The chart counts country-vaccine entries, not doses administered.

## 4. Business Recommendations

1. Review healthcare resource requirements in states with high confirmed case counts, such as Maharashtra.
2. Compare daily confirmed cases, recoveries, and deaths to identify periods requiring greater public-health attention.
3. Compare vaccination percentages across countries to identify differences in vaccination progress.
4. Consider population size when comparing total vaccination counts between countries.
5. Review recorded vaccine types across countries to understand the variety of vaccines included in the dataset.
6. Monitor infection trends and vaccination coverage together rather than relying on a single KPI.

These are proposed uses of the analysis, not measured improvements in public-health outcomes.

## 5. Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive overview of COVID-19 cases in India and global vaccination patterns.

The analysis highlights the scale of COVID-19, differences in state-wise case counts, changes in daily cases over time, vaccination counts across countries, and the distribution of recorded vaccine types.

These findings could help public-health teams review highly affected states, explore vaccination progress, and support healthcare resource planning.

**Note:** This analysis summarizes the available historical data. It does not establish the causes of case trends or measure the effectiveness of public-health decisions.

Vaccination doses represent recorded doses, not unique vaccinated people. The fully vaccinated percentage is an arithmetic average of available country-level percentages, not a population-weighted global rate. Vaccine-type counts represent country-vaccine entries, not doses administered.
